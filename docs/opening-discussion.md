# Try Supplier Website Readiness Planner and share what would make it more useful

This free browser tool is for aberdeen suppliers and service firms. Build a practical list of capability information, project evidence and documents to prepare for your website.

[Open the tool](https://web-design-aberdeen.github.io/supplier-document-register/). No coding knowledge, installation or account is needed. Start with the fictional example, or fill in the labelled fields with your own information. A buyer-information plan with specific gaps to fill before publishing.

Feedback about unclear questions, missing practical options or mobile usability is welcome here. Please use fictional information in public examples and keep customer or confidential details out of this discussion.

This organises your information. It does not verify certifications, approve supplier status or assess procurement compliance.

Maintained by [Aberdeen Web Design](https://webdesignaberdeen.uk/), operated by JW Digital Services Ltd. The original developer helpers are still available in the repository, but they are not needed to use the browser tool.
