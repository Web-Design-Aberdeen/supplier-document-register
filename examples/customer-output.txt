Example Aberdeen Supplier: supplier website plan

Prepare clear buyer information before investing in more website pages.

Who the website needs to help
- Supply: Inspection and reporting services
- Buyers: Facilities teams and procurement managers

Information already available
- Capability summary
- Current contact details

Gather or decide what is relevant
- Service specifications: prepare approved material or decide whether it is relevant.
- Approved project examples: prepare approved material or decide whether it is relevant.
- Accreditations: include only those you actually hold, with permission to display them.
- Coverage and delivery information: prepare approved material or decide whether it is relevant.

Buyer questions to answer
- Which areas do you cover?
- What information is needed for a quote?

Publishing responsibilities
- Information owner: Operations manager
- Scope and delivery limits: Availability and scope confirmed for each project.
- Keep document dates and versions visible. Review permission before publishing client names or project material.
